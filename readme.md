# 🚀 Skyline Sprint! 

Welcome to **Skyline Sprint**, an action-packed runner game where you zip across futuristic rooftops, dodge obstacles, outsmart rival runners, and collect glowing energy orbs! 🌟

---

## 🎮 How to Play
Your goal is simple: **Collect 12 Energy Orbs** while avoiding hazards!

* **`SPACEBAR`** – Jump high into the air! 🏃‍♂️💨
* **Mouse Clicks** – Use the menu buttons to start the game, toggle sound effects on/off, or exit.

> **Watch Out!** 
> * 🚧 **Road Barriers:** Jump right over them so you don't crash!
> * 👾 **Patrolling Rivals:** These sneaky characters move back and forth—don't let them catch you!

---
